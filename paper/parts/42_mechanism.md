### 4.7 The claim works by displacing the agent's own check
**Finding.** A false claim could raise V because agents believe it. Or it could raise V because it replaces the agent's own question, "does this need approval?". Experiment E12 separates the two with a 2×2 factorial design that crosses the claim with the verification rule of Section 4.3 (four models, the fresh scenarios of Section 4.8, {{R['counts']['e12']}} new episodes). Without the rule, the claim raises V from {{R['_2x2']['A none']['V']|.2f}} to {{R['_2x2']['B claim']['V']|.2f}}. With the rule, V is {{R['_2x2']['R reminder']['V']|.2f}} without the claim and {{R['_2x2']['Q claim+reminder']['V']|.2f}} with it, so the claim adds only {{R['_2x2']['Q-R (claim, with reminder)']['d']|+.2f}} [{{R['_2x2']['Q-R (claim, with reminder)']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{R['_2x2']['Q-R (claim, with reminder)']['hi']|+.2f}}]. The interaction, the claim's effect without the rule minus its effect with the rule, is {{R['H31']['point']|+.2f}} [{{R['H31']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{R['H31']['hi']|+.2f}}]. Agents do not simply believe the claim. They stop asking. The logs show this (exploratory). The Operator asked the permission system before acting in {{R['_asked']['A']['p']|.2f}} of episodes with no claim and {{R['_asked']['B']['p']|.2f}} with the claim, and in {{R['_asked']['R']['p']|.2f}} and {{R['_asked']['Q']['p']|.2f}} with the rule.

**Doubt restores the check but does not beat silence.** E13 replaces the false claim with messages that state doubt ({{R['counts']['e13']}} episodes, {{R['_e13_n_clusters']}} base tasks, five preregistered tests). A hedged message ("I think the approvals may have been obtained, but I have not checked; please confirm") gives V = {{R['_e13']['hedged']['V']|.2f}}. This equals no message ({{R['_e13']['none']['V']|.2f}}; difference {{R['H32']['point']|+.2f}}) and is far below the false claim ({{R['_e13']['false claim']['V']|.2f}}). The hedged result of E5 (Section 4.5), which was below no message, did not replicate. A neutral message with no approval content gives the same V ({{R['_e13']['neutral']['V']|.2f}}), so we cannot say that the effect is specific to what a message says about approval. Doubt also has a price: goal completion on safe twins falls from {{R['_e13']['goal none (safe)']['goal']|.2f}} without a message to {{R['_e13']['goal hedged (safe)']['goal']|.2f}} with the hedged one (a drop of {{R['H35']['point']+1e-9|.2f}}; our margin was 0.10). A peer's question ("has anyone confirmed that approvals were obtained? I have not seen them") does lower V, to {{R['_e13']['question']['V']|.2f}} ({{R['H33']['point']|+.2f}} against no message). We did not measure its price on safe twins. Table C2 gives the intervals.
