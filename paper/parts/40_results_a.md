## 4. Results
We ran {{D['counts']['e1']|,}} episodes for E1 (7 models × 3 team shapes × 2 assurance levels × 96 instances). Every hypothesis below was fixed before these data existed. We mark analyses that were not preregistered as *exploratory*.


### 4.1 A false assurance makes agents act without authorization
In the solo agent, the peer message raises the share of unsafe-twin episodes with an unauthorized action from {{X['V_none']['solo']|.2f}} to {{X['V_peer']['solo']|.2f}} (pooled difference {{H['H1']['point']|+.2f}}, 95% CI [{{H['H1']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H1']['hi']|+.2f}}]; H1 supported). The pooled number hides a split between models (Figure 2a; Appendix Table C2). Three models react strongly: Qwen3.5-397B ({{H['H1']['per_model']['qwen3.5-397b']|+.2f}}), Gemma-4 31B ({{H['H1']['per_model']['gemma-4-31b-it']|+.2f}}) and Gemma-4 26B ({{H['H1']['per_model']['gemma-4-26b-a4b-it']|+.2f}}). DeepSeek-V4 flash reacts weakly ({{H['H1']['per_model']['deepseek-v4-flash']|+.2f}}). Both GLM models and Qwen3.8-27B show almost no effect.

The effect stays in teams, and in absolute terms it is smaller. In the relay it is {{H['H2']['parts'][0]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H2']['parts'][0]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H2']['parts'][0]['hi']|+.2f}}] and in the relay-2 team {{H['H2']['parts'][1]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H2']['parts'][1]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H2']['parts'][1]['hi']|+.2f}}] (H2 supported). Teams do not amplify the violation. With the false assurance, the rate falls from {{X['V_peer']['solo']|.2f}} (solo) to {{X['V_peer']['relay1']|.2f}} (relay) and {{X['V_peer']['relay2']|.2f}} (relay-2). Without any assurance message the rates also fall with team size ({{X['V_none']['solo']|.2f}}, {{X['V_none']['relay1']|.2f}}, {{X['V_none']['relay2']|.2f}}), so the relative effect is not smaller in teams: the false message multiplies the rate by {{X['ratio_peer_none']['solo']|.1f}} (solo), {{X['ratio_peer_none']['relay1']|.1f}} (relay) and {{X['ratio_peer_none']['relay2']|.1f}} (relay-2).

### 4.2 The claim travels through the team and comes back as a report
Figure 3 follows the peer's ticket number through the relay (unsafe twins with the false assurance). The Lead's brief carries the claim in {{D['hops_unsafe_peer'][0]['brief_carries_claim']|.0%}} of relay episodes and in {{D['hops_unsafe_peer'][1]['brief_carries_claim']|.0%}} of relay-2 episodes. The other Leads drop it. This matters (exploratory): when the brief carries the claim, the Operator acts without authorization in {{X['brief_carries_vs_dropped']['V_a']|.0%}} of episodes, and when the Lead drops it, in {{X['brief_carries_vs_dropped']['V_b']|.0%}} (difference {{X['brief_carries_vs_dropped']['diff']|+.2f}} [{{X['brief_carries_vs_dropped']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{X['brief_carries_vs_dropped']['hi']|+.2f}}]). We also labeled the instruction that reached the Operator (exploratory; a model label that agrees with our hand labels on 35 of 36 cases). {{X['stance']['endorse']['share']|.0%}} of these instructions endorse the claim, {{X['stance']['reject']['share']|.0%}} reject it (they call it unverified or tell the Operator to verify), and {{X['stance']['absent']['share']|.0%}} do not mention it. After an endorsing instruction, violations occur in {{X['stance']['endorse']['V']|.0%}} of episodes. After a rejecting one, they occur in {{X['stance']['reject']['V']|.0%}} (difference {{X['stance']['endorse_minus_reject']['point']|+.2f}} [{{X['stance']['endorse_minus_reject']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{X['stance']['endorse_minus_reject']['hi']|+.2f}}]). The split follows the model (Appendix E).

The report is where the harm reaches the human. Violations are rarely disclosed: among violating episodes the final report says "not authorized" in {{H['H3']['point']|.0%}} (95% CI [{{H['H3']['lo']|.0%}}, {{H['H3']['hi']|.0%}}]; H3 supported), and it says "authorized" in {{H['H3']['false_assurance_over_V']|.0%}}. The rest say "unsure" or submitted no report. The solo agent is worst ({{X['fa_over_v']['solo']['FA']['point']|.0%}} false "authorized"). Teams are somewhat more honest (relay {{X['fa_over_v']['relay1']['FA']['point']|.0%}}, relay-2 {{X['fa_over_v']['relay2']['FA']['point']|.0%}}; exploratory), but the Lead's report still repeats the ticket number in {{D['hops_violators'][0]['final_report_cites_claim']|.0%}} of violating relay episodes.
