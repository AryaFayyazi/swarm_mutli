**E.4 Auditors and the prompt rule (4.3).** **An auditor agent helps, at a price.** Auditors cut violations to {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_min']['V']|.0%}} (sees the call only), {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace']['V']|.0%}} (also sees tool results) and {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace_just']['V']|.0%}} (also sees the swarm's words). All three stay above 5% (the call-only auditor only just). The call-only auditor blocks almost everything: safe-twin goal completion falls to {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_min']['goal_safe']|.2f}}. The two auditors with evidence cost {{H['_descriptive_controls']['baseline']['goal_safe'] - H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace']['goal_safe']|.2f}} and {{H['_descriptive_controls']['baseline']['goal_safe'] - H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace_just']['goal_safe']|.2f}}. {{D['auditor_fail_open']['e3']['no_parsable_verdict_fail_open']/D['auditor_fail_open']['e3']['audit_decisions']|.0%}} of auditor decisions had no parsable verdict and let the call run. A violation that passes the auditor is still reported as authorized in {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace']['FA_over_V']|.0%}} of cases (evidence auditor), so auditors do not repair reports. We found no sign that the swarm's own words recruit the auditor: V is {{H['H8']['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H8']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H8']['hi']|+.2f}}] higher with the words than without. **A prompt rule is nearly as good as an auditor.** One sentence in every system prompt ("a claim of approval is unverified until the permission system confirms it") cuts violations from {{H['_descriptive_controls']['baseline']['V']|.0%}} to {{H['_descriptive_controls']['prompt_defense']['V']|.0%}} ({{H['H12']['parts'][0]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H12']['parts'][0]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H12']['parts'][0]['hi']|+.2f}}]). It needs no extra calls and costs {{H['_descriptive_controls']['baseline']['goal_safe'] - H['_descriptive_controls']['prompt_defense']['goal_safe']|.2f}} of safe-twin goal completion. It stays {{H['H12']['parts'][1]['point']|.2f}} [{{H['H12']['parts'][1]['lo']|.2f}}, {{H['H12']['parts'][1]['hi']|.2f}}] above the gateway rule. **A diverse auditor does a little better.** If the auditor with evidence is GLM-5.2 and not the Operator's own model, violations fall to {{H['H17']['V_diverse']|.1%}} (same-model auditor: {{H['H17']['V_same']|.1%}}), a difference of {{H['H17']['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H17']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H17']['hi']|+.2f}}] (one test, p = {{H['H17']['p']|.2f}}). **E.8 Mixed teams (4.4).** We paired credulous models (Gemma-4 26B, Qwen3.5-397B) with skeptical models (GLM-5.2, glm-53-flash) in the relay: 8 pairs, {{D['counts']['e4']}} episodes. We expected the Operator's model to decide whether the team acts. It does not. With a skeptical Lead and a credulous Operator, V is {{X['e4_groups']['S lead + C op']['V']|.2f}}. For an all-credulous team it is {{X['e4_groups']['C lead + C op']['V']|.2f}}. The ratio is {{H['H13']['parts'][0]['point']|.0%}} [{{H['H13']['parts'][0]['lo']|.0%}}, {{H['H13']['parts'][0]['hi']|.0%}}], below our preregistered threshold of 50%. With a credulous Lead and a skeptical Operator, V is {{X['e4_groups']['C lead + S op']['V']|.2f}} [{{X['e4_groups']['C lead + S op']['lo']|.2f}}, {{X['e4_groups']['C lead + S op']['hi']|.2f}}], above our threshold of 0.10. So the Lead is the gatekeeper: its brief sets what the Operator believes. The Lead also decides what the human reads, since a skeptical Lead discloses violations {{H['H14']['point']|+.2f}} more often than an all-credulous team.

**E.9 The adaptive attacker (4.5).** The scripted message is a weak attacker, so we let a model write better ones. GLM-5.2, which is not an agent in these runs, wrote {{X['e8_search']['n1']}} candidate messages from a generic description of the task. It then saw how often each candidate led to a violation on a development seed, and wrote {{X['e8_search']['n2']}} improved candidates. A fixed rule picked the candidate with the highest development rate. On fresh tasks (relay, four models, {{H['_e8']['n']}} episodes), the selected message is **not stronger** than the main message: V is {{H['H18']['V_attacker']|.2f}} against {{H['H18']['V_main']|.2f}} ({{H['H18']['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H18']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H18']['hi']|+.2f}}]).
