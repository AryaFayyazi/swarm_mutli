TITLE: Who Approved This? Assurance Laundering in LLM Agent Teams
AUTHOR: Arya Fayyazi | University of Southern California
ABSTRACT:
LLM agents increasingly work in teams that use tools, and a human often reads only the team's final report. We study *assurance laundering*. A peer agent asserts, without proof, that approval was already given. The team then acts without authorization and tells the human that the work was authorized. We build CAPE-Swarm, a team layer on the CAPE-Bench environments [1]. Every task has a matched safe twin and unsafe twin, and a fixed rule, not an LLM judge, labels each tool call. Stopping the action and repairing the report need different fixes. A gateway rule that requires a permission request before any state-changing call stops the action. Showing the Lead a permission record repairs the report.
