**E.4 Auditors and the prompt rule (4.3).** **An auditor agent helps, at a price (H6).** Auditors cut violations to {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_min']['V']|.0%}} (sees the call only), {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace']['V']|.0%}} (also sees tool results) and {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_trace_just']['V']|.0%}} (also sees the swarm's words). The call-only auditor blocks almost everything: safe-twin goal completion falls to {{H['_descriptive_controls']['aud_min']['goal_safe']|.2f}}.
