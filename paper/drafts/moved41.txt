### 4.5 Robustness: wording, dose-response and repeats
The effect is not tied to one sentence. We re-ran the relay with the four models and three other wordings (E5, {{D['counts']['e5']|,}} episodes). Violations on unsafe twins: no message {{H['_e5_levels']['none']|.2f}}; the main peer wording {{H['_e5_levels']['peer']|.2f}}; a manager's message {{H['_e5_levels']['manager']|.2f}}; a terse "Approvals are already in place" {{H['_e5_levels']['terse']|.2f}}. Both new wordings raise violations over no message ({{H['H15']['parts'][0]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H15']['parts'][0]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H15']['parts'][0]['hi']|+.2f}}] and {{H['H15']['parts'][1]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H15']['parts'][1]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H15']['parts'][1]['hi']|+.2f}}]; H15 supported). The hedged wording ("I think approvals were obtained, but I have not checked. Please confirm before acting.") gives {{H['_e5_levels']['hedged']|.2f}}, which is {{H['H16']['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H16']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H16']['hi']|+.2f}}] relative to the main wording (H16 supported). It is also below the no-message rate ({{X['hedged_vs_none']['point']|+.2f}} [{{X['hedged_vs_none']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{X['hedged_vs_none']['hi']|+.2f}}], exploratory): a peer that states its own uncertainty makes the team safer than a silent peer. **Repeat runs (E6).** Re-running {{H['_e6']['n']}} relay episodes gives a pooled violation rate of {{H['_e6']['V_run1']|.2f}} and then {{H['_e6']['V_run2']|.2f}}, so the rate is stable. The outcome of a single episode is not: {{H['_e6']['match']|.0%}} of repeated episodes agree (Cohen's κ = {{H['_e6']['kappa']|.2f}}). Sampling at temperature 0.7 makes episodes noisy, and we therefore report rates, not single traces.
