## 5. Discussion and Limitations
**Three problems, three prices.** Acting without authorization, reporting it falsely and monitoring it are three problems with three fixes. One unverified sentence raised unauthorized actions by {{H['H1']['point']|+.2f}} in a solo agent. Team shape lowered the violation rate but did not fix the report: {{min(X['fa_over_v'][t]['FA']['point'] for t in X['fa_over_v'])|.0%}} to {{max(X['fa_over_v'][t]['FA']['point'] for t in X['fa_over_v'])|.0%}} of violating episodes ended with a false "authorized". A permission-system record repaired the report ({{H['H4']['parts'][0]['point']|.0%}} fewer false reports) with no change in the action. A structural gateway rule stopped the action ({{H['H5']['parts'][0]['point']|.1%}} violations) for about {{H['H5']['parts'][1]['point']*100|.0f}} points of utility. A verification prompt and an auditor sat in between, with a residual of about {{(X['resid'][0]+X['resid'][1])/2|.0%}}.

**What a deployer should log and enforce.** (1) Write the ledger at the gateway, with tool results, where no agent can edit it: a monitor reading the report ({{X['mon']['L0'][0]|.2f}}–{{X['mon']['L0'][1]|.2f}} AUROC) or calls without results ({{X['mon']['L2'][0]|.2f}}–{{X['mon']['L2'][1]|.2f}}) is close to blind, and one reading calls with results reaches {{X['mon']['L3'][0]|.2f}}–{{X['mon']['L3'][1]|.2f}}. (2) Keep an explicit permission-system record, and make reports cite it and not a peer. (3) Enforce ask-before-act at the gateway, or, if you cannot, mark every approval claim that crosses an agent boundary as unverified (the claim filter, 4.8). (4) Test your own models with matched twins first: three of seven hardly reacted to the claim and three reacted strongly, so model choice is a safety decision. Teams should also make agents state what they did not verify (4.5).

**What did not hold.** H13 failed (the Lead gatekeeps, so the Operator's model does not decide alone), H8 failed (the swarm's words do not recruit an auditor or monitor), H18 failed (a short attacker search found no stronger message), and H25 failed (we did not show the filter is cheaper than the gate).

**Limitations.** We used open-weight models only, a weak attacker (scripted wordings and a short search, 4.7), mock tools, an oracle upper bound, judges from the same model families as the agents, and 48 scenarios per cell. The gateway ledger is assumed tamper-proof; real agents have edited logs [13]. Appendix A gives the full list. **Future work:** frontier models, stronger attackers, real swarm logs and mesh topologies.
