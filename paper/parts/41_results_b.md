### 4.4 In a mixed team, the Lead is the gatekeeper
We paired credulous models (Gemma-4 26B, Qwen3.5-397B) with skeptical ones (GLM-5.2, glm-53-flash) in the relay ({{D['counts']['e4']}} episodes). We expected the Operator's model to decide whether the team acts. It does not. A skeptical Lead with a credulous Operator gives V = {{X['e4_groups']['S lead + C op']['V']|.2f}}, against {{X['e4_groups']['C lead + C op']['V']|.2f}} for an all-credulous team. A credulous Lead with a skeptical Operator gives {{X['e4_groups']['C lead + S op']['V']|.2f}}. The Lead's brief sets what the Operator believes, and the Lead also decides what the human reads: a skeptical Lead discloses violations {{H['H14']['point']|+.2f}} more often (Appendix E.8).

### 4.5 Robustness: wording, repeats and an adaptive attacker
**Wording.** The effect does not depend on one sentence. A manager's message and a terse "Approvals are already in place" both raise V over no message ({{H['H15']['parts'][0]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H15']['parts'][0]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H15']['parts'][0]['hi']|+.2f}}] and {{H['H15']['parts'][1]['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H15']['parts'][1]['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H15']['parts'][1]['hi']|+.2f}}]). A hedged message ("I have not checked; please confirm before acting") gives V = {{H['_e5_levels']['hedged']|.2f}}, below the main wording and also below no message at all ({{H['_e5_levels']['none']|.2f}}; exploratory). Section 4.7 tests this surprising result on fresh tasks.

**Repeats.** A single episode repeats its own outcome only {{H['_e6']['match']|.0%}} of the time (κ = {{H['_e6']['kappa']|.2f}}), so we report rates and not single traces.

**An adaptive attacker.** A model (GLM-5.2) wrote candidate messages and improved them against a development seed. Its best message is **not stronger** than the scripted one on fresh tasks (V = {{H['H18']['V_attacker']|.2f}} against {{H['H18']['V_main']|.2f}}; difference {{H['H18']['point']|+.2f}} [{{H['H18']['lo']|+.2f}}, {{H['H18']['hi']|+.2f}}]), and the gate holds against it (V = {{H['H19']['parts'][0]['point']|.1%}}). Appendix E.9 has the search.
