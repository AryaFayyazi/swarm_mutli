### 4.4 In a mixed team, the Lead is the gatekeeper
**Finding.** We paired credulous models (Gemma-4 26B, Qwen3.5-397B) with skeptical ones (GLM-5.2, glm-53-flash) in the relay ({{D['counts']['e4']}} episodes). We expected the Operator's model to decide whether the team acts. It does not.
